Helvetica Works

Our Story

Helvetica Works Sdn. Bhd. specialises in exhibitions, corporate events, product launches, roadshows and festive decoration. Its work also spans props, interior works and large-format printing.

The company brings together in-house creative staff, including designers and marketing specialists, alongside production expertise to carry ideas through to execution.
