Project Stories

From brief to opening night

Read Article

Start with the change you want the audience to experience. A launch may need one memorable reveal, while a conference may need a layout that supports a full day of talks and networking.

Turn that objective into practical decisions about sightlines, lighting and stage access. Schedule a rehearsal to check cues and confirm who owns each handover before guests arrive.

Behind the Scenes

Inside a production setup

Read Article

Build a run sheet that links setup tasks to venue access. Confirm loading times, installation order and equipment handover with every team involved.

Walk the space before opening. Check visitor routes, presentation cues and the condition of visible finishes, then give on-site teams a clear point of contact for last-minute changes.

Industry Insights

Designing an inviting booth

Read Article

Decide what visitors should notice first from the aisle. A strong focal point, a legible message and a clear entrance help visitors understand the booth as they approach.

Map where people will pause for a demo or a conversation. Leave room for queues and keep circulation clear, then use lighting to distinguish hands-on displays from quieter discussion areas.
